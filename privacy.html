Privacy Policy

lemmetalktech uploader · Last updated 16 November 2026

Summary

lemmetalktech uploader is a private tool that uploads our own videos to our own TikTok account, @lemmetalktech, as drafts. It does not collect, sell or share anyone's personal data.

What the app stores

  • The sign-in token TikTok gives us when we connect our own account. It is kept only on our own computer and is used only to upload drafts and to check whether a draft was posted.
  • Basic account details TikTok returns with that sign-in (our account's ID). We use them only to know which account the drafts go to.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect any data about viewers, followers or other TikTok users.
  • It does not use cookies, analytics or advertising.
  • It does not sell or share data with anyone.

Removing access

We can disconnect the app at any time in the TikTok app's settings (Security and permissions, Apps and services), and we delete the stored token from our computer when we stop using the app.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
